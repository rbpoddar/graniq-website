01 · Application

Kitchen countertops& vanities

Heat-proof, scratch-resistant surfaces that stay beautiful for decades.

Granite worktops handle hot pans, knives and daily cleaning without fuss. We supply polished, honed or leathered slabs in 2 cm and 3 cm, plus vanity tops, splashbacks and island pieces cut to your drawings.

  • Wire-saw slabs, shade-matched for book-matched islands
  • Cut-to-size worktops, edges and sink cut-outs on request
  • Vanity tops, splashbacks and window sills

Each kitchen shows a stone from our current slab stock. Each slab is unique; please approve the actual slabs or bundle photos before ordering.

Monuments& memorials

A lasting tribute, made to be visited for generations.

Granite keeps a name, a story and a quiet place of remembrance. Dense, frost-resistant stone holds its polish and lettering through every season.

  • Headstones & tablets
  • Grave covers & ledgers
  • Kerb sets, single & double
  • Bases, plinths & vases
  • Lanterns & plaques
  • Urn graves & columbaria

Shapes and dimensions to your drawing or standard set list. Polished, honed, flamed and rock-pitched details on request.

Outdoor& landscaping

Frost-proof stone for terraces, steps, streets and facades.

Flamed, bush-hammered or sandblasted granite gives lasting grip and survives freeze-thaw winters. We cut paving, setts, kerbs, steps and cladding panels to size.

  • Paving slabs, setts and cobbles
  • Steps, risers, copings and kerbs
  • Ventilated and fixed facade cladding
